Our story

We were tired of being tired the wrong way.

It started with a simple frustration: every energy product forced a trade-off. Coffee meant a bathroom break and an afternoon crash. Energy drinks meant sugar and a racing heart. Pills meant guessing the dose. Nothing respected the body it was fueling.

So a pharmacologist and an ultramarathoner spent two years in the lab perfecting buccal delivery — the same fast-absorption path used in modern medicine — and paired it with L-theanine to keep the energy smooth. The result is a pouch that works in seconds and never asks for the crash back.

  • Manufactured in an FDA-registered, GMP-certified facility
  • Plant-derived caffeine, fully vegan and gluten-free
  • Carbon-neutral shipping and recyclable tins
